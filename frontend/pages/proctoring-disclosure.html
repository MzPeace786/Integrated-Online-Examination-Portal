Academic Integrity & Computer Vision Methodology Disclosure
Important Ethical & Technical Notice: The automated proctoring features within ExamPortal are computer vision heuristics designed to assist human examiners by generating suspicious-activity indicators. The system does not definitively determine cheating, and no autonomous academic penalties are enacted without manual reviewer verification.
1. Technical Detection Capabilities

During an active proctored examination, your webcam stream captures periodic frames (at ~5-second intervals) which are evaluated in-memory by our Python OpenCV service for:

  • Candidate Face Presence: Detects whether the candidate's face is centered in the camera frame using Haar cascade frontal classifiers.
  • Multiple Face Identification: Identifies if additional individuals appear within the field of view.
  • Gaze Shift Heuristics: Analyzes eye landmark presence and facial aspect ratios to detect extended periods of looking away from the assessment interface.
  • Camera Status Integrity: Identifies blacked out or obstructed camera feeds.
2. Browser Anti-Cheating Controls

The browser interface monitors environmental security parameters including:

  • Enforced Full-Screen mode with auto-logging of exit events
  • Tab-switching and window blur event tracking
  • Clipboard copy/paste restrictions
3. Data Lifecycle & Privacy Guarantees

We prioritize candidate data minimization under Privacy by Design:

  • No Continuous Video Storage: Raw webcam video is never stored continuously. Only numerical indicator metadata and severity classifications are recorded.
  • Explicit Prior Consent: The webcam is activated strictly after you grant browser permissions and submit required pre-exam consent check gates.
  • Review Rights: You can view all recorded proctoring events associated with your examination attempts in your Student Privacy Dashboard.
