Examination Terms & Conditions

Last Updated: October 2026
1. Examination Rules & Integrity

Candidates must complete assessments independently without unauthorized physical or digital aids. All browser departures, multiple faces, and camera obstructions will be logged for review.

2. Technical Pre-requisites

A functional web camera, supported modern browser, and uninterrupted internet connectivity are required for proctored examinations.

3. Certificate Issuance

Certificates of completion are digitally generated upon meeting or exceeding the minimum passing percentage set by the examiner.
