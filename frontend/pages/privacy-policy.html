Platform Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 • Effective Date: October 2026 • Department of Computer Science and Engineering
1. Information We Collect

We collect account information (name, institutional email, department, roll number), assessment responses, and computer vision proctoring metrics generated during active examinations.

2. Purpose of Data Processing

Your data is processed strictly for authenticating candidates, providing examination interfaces, calculating assessment marks, issuing verifiable certificates, and providing examiners with audit logs to ensure academic fairness.

3. Webcam and Biometric Processing

Webcam frames are processed in-memory for face detection and suspicious behavior indicators. We do not permanently record continuous video feeds. Snapshot capture is disabled by default and requires explicit administrator configuration with prior student notice.

4. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

Student account records and certificates are retained based on institutional policies. You may inspect your personal records or submit a formal data deletion request through the Student Privacy Dashboard.

5. Contact Information

For any privacy or data protection inquiries, contact the examination administrator at privacy@examportal.com.
